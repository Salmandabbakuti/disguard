Discord verification

Verify your wallet.

Connect the wallet that holds the required token, then sign a message to prove you own it. This does not create a transaction or cost gas.

We will check your balance against this server’s requirement and assign its Discord role if you qualify.

How it works

Three steps from setup to access.

Configure the rule in Discord. Members prove wallet ownership. DisGuard checks the balance and assigns the role.

  1. 01Admin: configure the ruleInstall DisGuard on your Discord server, move its bot role above the gated role in Server Settings → Roles, then run /set-serverconfig to configure the RPC URL, token address, minimum balance, start channel, and role.
  2. 02Member: prove ownershipRun /verify in the configured start channel. Open the link, connect a wallet, and sign a message. No transaction is sent and no gas is charged.
  3. 03DisGuard: check and assignDisGuard verifies the signature, checks the wallet’s ERC-20 or ERC-721 balance through the configured RPC, and assigns the Discord role when the minimum is met. That role unlocks the gated channels.
